# Repair pricing sources — 7 November 2026

The owner authorised £60 back-glass repairs for iPhone 11–14 families, £75 for iPhone 15–16 families and £85 for later priced models. All existing iPhone screen and battery prices remain unchanged. iPhone 18 and Duo stay quote-only because the requested iSmash benchmark was not published for those models at the time checked.

For other listed devices, iTop prices are 90% of the exact model/repair/variant list price, rounded to the nearest penny. The snapshot in src/benchmarkRepairPrices.mjs retains the original price, originating product and collection URLs, variant IDs, title and checked date for each of 509 options across 127 models (62 Samsung/Google phones, 28 iPads and 37 MacBook models).

Sources were discovered through these official iSmash directories and their public Shopify product listings:
- https://www.ismash.com/pages/samsung-repairs
- https://www.ismash.com/pages/google-repairs
- https://www.ismash.com/pages/ipad-repairs
- https://www.ismash.com/pages/new-macbook-template

Only exact catalogue models and explicit source groupings are mapped. A52/A32 4G/5G and A51 4G/5G retain their source-listed network coverage. Galaxy S20 FE prices explicitly apply to 5G only. Inner/outer screens, main/base batteries, wide/ultra-wide cameras, storage variants, MacBook A-numbers/years/EMCs and separate frame charges remain distinct. Front-glass digitiser prices do not represent a complete LCD display repair. Diagnostic charges are assessment only.

The zero-valued Pixel 10a battery listing is excluded. Empty collections and repairs with no published price stay manual quotes. Source stock flags do not imply iTop stock. No competitor genuine-part, warranty, turnaround or availability promises were copied. Part selection and availability must be confirmed with iTop.

This is a checked pricing snapshot, not continuous price monitoring or an ongoing comparison-price guarantee. No background refresh has been set up. Online bookings and payments remain disabled while the provider and booking service are being connected.
